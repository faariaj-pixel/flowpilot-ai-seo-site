# SEO Strategy: FlowPilot AI

## Business positioning
FlowPilot AI is positioned as a Toronto-based AI automation consulting, implementation and training partner for SMBs that need measurable workflow improvement rather than generic AI advice. The strongest commercial association is: FlowPilot AI -> AI automation consultant -> SMB implementation -> Toronto/Ontario -> clinics, real estate, law firms, accounting firms, home services, ecommerce and professional services.

## SEO objectives
- Generate qualified inbound opportunities from buyers actively looking for AI automation consulting, implementation, pricing, training, workshops, quotes or local support.
- Prioritize searches with high buying intent and clear SMB business value over vanity traffic.
- Use the free AI workflow audit as the primary conversion path.

## Ideal customers
1. Clinics needing intake, reminders and admin routing.
2. Real estate teams needing lead response and follow-up.
3. Law firms needing consultation intake and document workflows.
4. Accounting firms needing onboarding, deadline and reporting workflows.
5. Home services needing quote, dispatch and review follow-up.
6. Ecommerce brands needing customer service, ops and reporting automation.
7. Professional services firms needing repeatable client delivery operations.

## Search opportunity analysis
Highest buying intent is concentrated around local consultant/provider searches and implementation/pricing searches. Broader authority opportunities include AI workflow automation, business process automation for SMBs, AI agents for business operations, AI intake automation, AI lead follow-up automation and AI automation ROI.

## Topical authority strategy
Pillar: AI automation for SMBs.
Commercial pages: AI automation consulting, AI automation implementation, AI automation training/workshops, AI automation pricing.
Use cases: lead intake, follow-up, onboarding, reporting, customer service, operations.
Industries: clinics, real estate, law firms, accounting firms, home services, ecommerce and professional services.
Authority content: ROI guide, cost guide, implementation checklist, AI automation examples, AI workflow architecture, tool comparisons.

## Local SEO strategy
Primary location: Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Secondary local signals: GTA, North York, Downtown Toronto, Mississauga, Markham, Vaughan, Scarborough, Etobicoke, Brampton, Oakville and Ontario. Avoid thin location spam; create unique pages only for locations with clear customer relevance.

## Internal linking strategy
Authority content -> problem/use-case sections -> commercial service pages -> audit CTA. The homepage and navigation link to the highest-value commercial and local pages.

## AEO/GEO strategy
Use direct definitions, FAQs, comparison-ready language, clear service lists, entity schema, location/service relationships and dated evergreen resources so AI discovery systems can identify and cite FlowPilot AI accurately.

## Authority-building strategy
Add real case studies, screenshots, before/after workflow maps, client outcomes, testimonials, founder bio, training materials, original ROI calculators and named implementation frameworks when available. Do not invent proof.
