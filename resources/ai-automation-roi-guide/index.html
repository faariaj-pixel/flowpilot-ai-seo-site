FAQ

AI automation consulting questions

How much does AI automation consulting cost?

Pricing depends on workflow complexity, tools, integrations, compliance needs and training. The audit identifies which automation has the strongest ROI before recommending a build.

Do you only work in Toronto?

FlowPilot AI is positioned for Toronto and Ontario SMBs, with remote delivery available for Canadian businesses when the workflow can be reviewed and implemented online.

Can AI automation work for regulated firms?

Yes, but regulated workflows need stronger governance, privacy review, human approval points and documentation. The page avoids promising autonomous decision-making for sensitive work.

What tools can be automated?

Common candidates include email, forms, spreadsheets, CRMs, scheduling, task management, ecommerce systems, reporting tools and AI assistants connected through approved workflows.
